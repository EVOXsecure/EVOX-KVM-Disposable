What is this?

EVOX gives you a throw-away computer inside your computer. We call it the box. When you are done, everything in it is gone.

Before and after

Everything that happens in the box disappears with it.

Why is it safe?

The box is sealed off. It cannot reach your home network or your own files. When you finish, the box is destroyed, and at the next start you get a fresh one.

3 golden rules

  1. Always click Stop and erase. Closing the window is not enough.
  2. Do not keep important passwords in the box.
  3. For documents, use OFFLINE.

Be honest

What you type into the box can be stolen, for example a password on a fake website. EVOX protects your computer. It does not protect you from your own mistakes.
